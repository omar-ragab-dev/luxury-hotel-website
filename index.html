frequently asked questions

how to cancel?

You can cancel your reservation free of charge up to 48 hours before check-in through our website or by contacting our front desk.

is there any vacancy?

Yes, we have rooms available throughout the year. We recommend booking early during peak seasons to secure your preferred room type.

what are payment methods?

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, bank transfers, and cash payments at the front desk.

how to claim coupons codes?

Simply enter your coupon code at checkout on our booking page. The discount will be applied automatically to your total.

what are the age requirements?

Guests must be at least 18 years old to book a room. Children under 12 stay free when sharing a room with parents.
